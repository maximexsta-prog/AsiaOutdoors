FAQ

See our FAQ page
1. DWS : HOW HIGH DO WE HAVE TO JUMP FROM?

It's up to you! You can feel safe and comfortable in this professional team environment. Your guide will push you to climb to your limit but you will never feel pressured.

2. I'M PLANNING A TRIP. WHEN WILL THE TIDES BE RIGHT?

Contact us before your departure to make sure we can get you out. You wouldn't want to miss this awesome experience.

3. CAN I GO DEEPING BY MYSELF?

It is highly recommended that you only join a DWS trip with a licensed operator for safety reasons. Langur's adventures has been granted a license from Cat Ba National Park to run such trips. VietClimb from Hanoi also has a climbing license and the necessary expertise. Hiring an independent basket boat to take you deeping is both dangerous and illegal! Most basket boat drivers do not understand the practices required to safely Deep Water Solo. Additionally, they do not know which walls have water deep enough (and clear of hazards) for a safe landing, and do not know when tides are right.

4. DOES THE PRICE INCLUDE GEAR RENTAL?

Yes! All prices include gear, guides, harbor entry tickets, lunch, and transport to and from location.

5. CAN I BELAY ON A TOP-ROPE TRIP?

No. Due to safety standards and liability concerns, only guides are allowed to belay on top-rope trips.

6. WHAT SHOULD I WEAR?

For kayaking on cold days: We suggest either swimsuit and shorts or waterproof pants, with a long-sleeved shirt or jacket. If you bring a jacket with metal pieces in the zipper, be sure to rinse it with fresh water when you get back to the island!

For kayaking on warm days: Swimsuit, shorts, t-shirt, and hat!

For climbing: Clothing that is comfortable to move in. Long pants may help inexperienced climbers avoid small scrapes from the rock.
*If you are kayaking in the morning and climbing in the afternoon, we ask that you bring a clean set of clothes to climb in. This will be more comfortable for you and helps us minimize exposing our climbing gear to salt water, keeping it in operational shape for longer.
