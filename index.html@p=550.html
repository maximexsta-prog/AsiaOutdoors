Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT'S THE WEATHER LIKE TOMORROW?
September to December
The best time to visit Cat Ba is from late September to November, when skies tend to be clear.  Temperatures cool during the winter to 15-20 C, and an eerie mist settles over the karst limestone. 
 
February to May
Rain is common during these months.
 
June to August
Rain continues into these months.  Average temperature is 25 to 28 C, although temperatures often reach 30 C, with high humidity.  
Although it is difficult to predict the weather on Cat Ba in the short term, some weather stations try anyway.  Be aware that whether it says sunshine or rain, there are never any guarantees.
WHERE DO YOUR TRIPS LEAVE FROM?

Langurs adventures begin and end at the Langurs adventures office in Cat Ba Town, on Cat Ba Island. Trip prices include all transportation for the trip's duration. However, transport to/from Hanoi or boat tickets to/from Hai Phong are not included. ​​

DO YOU GO TO HA LONG BAY?

Our trips run in Lan Ha Bay, separated from Ha Long Bay by a single channel of water. ​

WHAT'S THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN HA LONG BAY AND LAN HA BAY?

Lan Ha Bay is simply one area of the larger "Ha Long Bay region." When referred to in this way, the HLB region includes Ha Long Bay, Lan Ha Bay, Cat Ba Island and Bai Tu Long Bay. Many tourists prefer Lan Ha Bay due to its peaceful waters and relaxed atmosphere. The landscape, beauty, and karst formations of Lan Ha Bay are just as stunning as those found in Ha Long Bay. Lan Ha Bay also offers more exposure to local fishing life, and you're less likely to see as many tour boats and large ships passing though.

DO I NEED TO BOOK A TRIP IN ADVANCE?

Although booking in advance isn't required, it can certainly be a good idea as trips tend to fill fast. In order to allow processing time, Booking Forms must be submitted at least one week in advance of the trip requested and must be paid at least 48 hours prior to the start of your trip in order to reserve your place. Otherwise, you're always welcome to come into our office on Cat Ba and book in person as long as we have space available. We'll do our best to find an adventure for you!

DO YOU RUN BAY CRUISES AND SAILING TRIPS?

All of our trips are activity-based. We cruise through the bay on a large traditional Junk Boat to access our climbing and kayaking locations. In this sense, our trips include a cruise that takes you as far as an hour into Lan Ha Bay, giving a stunning introduction to the region. However, we do not run independent full-day cruises or sailing trips. ​

CAN I RENT A KAYAK WITHOUT A GUIDE?

No. Lan Ha Bay is an island maze, where changing tides close passages and thick mist can develop without warning. Even with a compass, it can be extremely difficult to navigate among islands. For this reason, all of our kayak trips are guided.

CAN YOU WORK WITH DIETARY RESTRICTIONS?

Yes! Let us know what you need and we will work with you.

WHAT FORMS OF CURRENCY DO YOU ACCEPT?

Vietnamese Dong, US Dollars, Mastercard, and VISA (and AMEX for online payments).

DO YOU CANCEL TRIPS IF IT'S RAINING?

Our trips run every day, rain or shine, except in the event of hazardous weather such as typhoons. Because our climbing walls are sheltered, we can even climb when it's raining!

WHAT IF I CAN'T SWIM?

We provide PFDs for every person on our trips!

WHERE DO I LEAVE MY GEAR WHILE I'M ON A TRIP?

If you've checked out of your hotel, feel free to stash your bags in our office while you're on our trip for the day. If you're on one of our overnights, we'll lock up your gear when we close for the evening and have it waiting for you when you return to the island!

IS THERE A SAFE PLACE FOR ME TO STORE VALUABLES?

Yes. We have a safe in the office where we can lock up valuables such as cell phones, wallets and passports and guarantee their safety while you're on our trips. Any valuables you bring onto the trip are your responsibility to protect, therefore we highly recommend leaving all valuables at our office.

WHAT IF MY BUS LEAVES THE ISLAND BEFORE THE TRIP GETS BACK?

Let us know what your itinerary is, and we can probably work with you in order to arrange a modified excursion that will accommodate your travel plans.
